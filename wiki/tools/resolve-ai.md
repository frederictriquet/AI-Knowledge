# Resolve.ai

**In one sentence** — platform of **AI production / on-call agents**: agents take on on-call duty, incidents and daily production work, with the engineer directing and validating actions.

## Type & integration
**Proprietary SaaS, enterprise-oriented.** Teams of agents investigate **with** your engineers (triage, RCA of complex problems). Enterprise security: SAML SSO, RBAC, redaction/encryption, per-org isolation, activity logs, **no model training** on your data. Cited customers: Coinbase, DoorDash, Expedia, Snowflake, MongoDB. Valued ~$1B (Dec 2025), Splunk founder pedigree.

## Pricing model
**Proprietary, enterprise** — no public price, **by quote**. *(observed 2026-06-18)*

## LLM cost
**Included (📦)** — inference is **provided by Resolve.ai** as part of the enterprise contract; no BYOK or token billing shown on the customer side.

## What it's for
Heavily automate on-call and incident response (announced target: up to ~80% auto-resolution, with guardrails). The human "steps in" to direct/act. Target: large organizations with a high incident load.

- **[CI/CD, delivery & ops](../guides/generate-code-with-ai.md#fam-ci-cd-delivery-ai-assisted-operations) family**, **AI SRE** sub-space; the most **aggressive on autonomy** of the trio ([Cleric](cleric.md) = cautious/read-only, [Traversal](traversal.md) = precision/ML).

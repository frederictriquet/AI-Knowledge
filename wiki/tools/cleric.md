# Cleric

**In one sentence** — **AI SRE** agent that continuously investigates production incidents, produces the *root cause analysis* and recommends fixes, by plugging into your observability stack.

## Type & integration
**Proprietary SaaS platform**. Connects to the stack (AWS, Azure, Datadog, Grafana, PagerDuty…) "in an afternoon". **Read access by default, write when you're ready**; every action is logged and auditable. Acts as the team's "operational memory". Gartner *Cool Vendor 2025* (AI for SRE & Observability).

## Pricing model
**Proprietary, SaaS.** No public pricing or free tier shown → **demo / enterprise** (on quote). *(observed 2026-06-18)*

## LLM cost
**Included (📦)** — Cleric **runs the LLM in its service**; you bring no key. The LLM cost is built into the contract (no token billing visible on the client side). A **read-only by default** posture = no destructive action without your approval.

## What it's for
Reduce MTTR: correlate logs/metrics/traces to diagnose in minutes, keep an auditable RCA. Claimed: ~5 min to root cause, 92% "actionable" findings, 200,000+ investigations.

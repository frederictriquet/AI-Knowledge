# Relay.app

**In one sentence** — Workflow automation platform that stands out for **human-in-the-loop**: insert human validation/judgment steps in the middle of automations, with an AI assistant that builds flows in natural language.

## Type & integration
Proprietary SaaS. An AI assistant assembles automations from a description; many integrations; **intermediate manual steps** (review, approval, human context) — the differentiator vs Zapier. Integrates GPT, Claude, Gemini (runs on the latest Claude models).

## Pricing model
Proprietary, freemium (observed on 2026-06-17, annual billing):
- **Free**: $0 — 1 user, 200 steps/month, 500 AI credits/month.
- **Professional**: $19/month — 750 steps, 2,000 AI credits.
- **Team**: $59/month — 10 users, 1,500 steps, 2,000 AI credits.
- **Enterprise**: by quote.

## LLM cost
**📦 Included (AI credits) or 🔑 BYOK**: AI credits included in each tier; the option to plug in your own keys as an alternative.

## What it's for
Automate processes **where the human must stay in control** of certain decisions (approvals, checks) — a niche that fully automatic tools cover poorly. Neighbor of Gumloop, Zapier AI, Make.

## Notes
- Native HITL is the key argument; relevant for high-stakes workflows (finance, legal, ops).
- Steps + AI credits model → estimate the monthly volume.
- ⚠️ Closed proprietary SaaS (lock-in, no self-host); the steps + AI credits model can become costly at scale — size the monthly volume before committing. The "differentiator vs Zapier" is a vendor argument.

# Gumloop

**In one sentence** — **No-code** AI automation platform: you assemble workflows and agents (without code) to automate business tasks, on a credit model.

## Type & integration
Proprietary SaaS (raised $50M in Series B, Benchmark). No-code builder for workflows/agents with many integrations; consumption measured in **credits**. AI model access control on Enterprise.

## Pricing model
Proprietary, freemium (observed 2026-06-17):
- **Free**: 5,000 credits/month.
- **Pro**: $37/month (20,000+ credits).
- **Enterprise**: on quote.
- Credits **scale** with need (5k → 1.5M+).

## LLM cost
**📦 Included (via credits) or 🔑 BYOK**: by default, AI usage is covered by the platform's credits (predictable cost); the **"Bring Your Own API Keys"** option lets you plug in your own providers.

## What it's for
Automating **business** processes with AI without coding (scraping, document processing, enrichment, agents). Targets non-dev users. Neighbour of Relay.app, Zapier AI, Make.

## Notes
- **Credit** model: monitor real consumption (AI steps consume credits fast).
- Proprietary no-code → lock-in vs open-source builders (Flowise, Sim).

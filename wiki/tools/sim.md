# Sim (Sim Studio)

**In one sentence** — an open-source (YC) platform for **building and orchestrating AI agent workflows** via a drag-and-drop visual canvas; 1,000+ integrations and all major LLMs, in the cloud or self-hosted.

## Type & integration
Visual canvas to build, deploy and orchestrate agents/workflows (your AI's "central intelligence layer"). 1,000+ integrations, support for OpenAI / Anthropic Claude / Gemini / Mistral / xAI. Managed cloud or self-hosting.

## Pricing model
**Open-source — Apache 2.0**, self-host free. Cloud (observed 2026-06-17): free community plan (~1,000 credits); paid ~**$20–25/month** (~6,000 credits). YC-backed, 100k+ builders claimed.

## LLM cost
**🔑 BYOK**: you plug in your own provider keys (all major LLMs supported); Sim does not intermediate token billing.

## What it's for
Visually building agentic workflows connected to many tools, like an "agent factory" — between no-code business tools (Gumloop) and code frameworks (LangGraph). Direct neighbor of Flowise.

## Notes
- Apache 2.0 → frictionless self-host if you want to keep your data.
- Young product but claimed traction; compare with Flowise/Dify depending on required integrations.

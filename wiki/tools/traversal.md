# Traversal

**In one sentence** — "the AI SRE for complex systems": causal incident investigation at scale (petabytes, 1.7M+ nodes in demo), alert triage, RCA and self-healing.

## Type & integration
**Proprietary SaaS** with a **BYOC option** (Bring Your Own Cloud — runs in your infra, a security argument). In-house technologies: **Production World Model™** (dependency mapping) and **Causal Search Engine™** (RCA by tracing incidents ↔ changes). Connects to monitoring, incident tools, **Git repos** (GitHub) and production telemetry; natural-language queries.

## Pricing model
**Proprietary, enterprise / on quote**; BYOC for "security-first" organizations. *(observed 2026-06-18)*

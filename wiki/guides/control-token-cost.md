# ⚡ Mastering token cost

> **Objective guide (L3)** — how do you cut the cost (and latency) of an LLM system without losing quality?
> Concepts/practices below; **tools** (token optimization, gateways/routers) in the **Tools section** at the bottom of the page.

## In brief

Cost is worked at several levels: **pick the right model** (routing/cascade), **don't recompute** (caching), **emit fewer wasted tokens** (constrained outputs), **go faster** (speculative decoding) and **shrink the injected context**. Most of these are silent on quality — which is what makes them high-impact levers.

## Suggested reading path

1. **Route & cascade** — [Model routing & cascades](../concepts/model-routing-cascades.md): send each request to the cheapest model *capable* of handling it.
2. **Don't recompute** — [Prompt caching](../concepts/prompt-caching.md) and [semantic caching](../concepts/semantic-caching.md).
3. **Emit less** — [constrained decoding / structured output](../concepts/constrained-decoding.md) and [structured outputs (instructor / Pydantic)](../concepts/structured-outputs-instructor.md).
4. **Go faster** — [Speculative decoding](../concepts/speculative-decoding.md).
5. **Shrink the context** — [Contextual Retrieval](../concepts/contextual-retrieval.md) for chunks that are more relevant and fewer in number.
6. **Trade off reasoning** — [reasoning models & test-time compute](../concepts/inference-time-scaling.md): pay for compute at inference when (and only when) it is worth it.

### 🧠 Reasoning & planning
- 🔴 **[Reasoning models & test-time compute](../concepts/inference-time-scaling.md)** — gain quality by letting the model "think longer" at inference rather than by growing its weights.

### ✍️ Prompting
- 🟡 **[Prompt caching](../concepts/prompt-caching.md)** — reusing an already-computed response for an identical prompt, but beware: the tutorial implements an exact-match response cache on the client side (LangChain `SQLiteCache`), not provider-side prefix prompt caching (KV-cache).

### 📊 Evaluation
- 🟡 **[Contextual Retrieval](../concepts/contextual-retrieval.md)** — prefix each chunk with a short context (situating the chunk in its document) *before* embedding, to reduce retrieval failures caused by ambiguous chunks.

### ⚡ Efficiency & cost
- 🟡 **[Constrained decoding / structured output](../concepts/constrained-decoding.md)** — force the output to respect a grammar/schema (JSON, regex) by masking invalid tokens at decoding time; guarantees a parsable format (≠ "politely asking" for JSON).
- 🟡 **[Model routing & cascades](../concepts/model-routing-cascades.md)** — route each request to the cheapest model CAPABLE of handling it, or chain from small to large (cascade) with a confidence judge; sharply reduces cost at near-constant quality.
- 🟡 **[Semantic caching](../concepts/semantic-caching.md)** — cache queries, context and results by semantic similarity, used as an agent memory mechanism.
- 🟡 **[Speculative decoding](../concepts/speculative-decoding.md)** — a small "draft" model proposes several tokens, the large model VERIFIES them in one pass; speeds up inference without changing the output distribution.
- 🟡 **[Structured outputs (instructor / Pydantic)](../concepts/structured-outputs-instructor.md)** — get typed and validated data from an LLM (via Pydantic models) rather than parsing free text, with automatic validation and retries.

### LLM gateways / routers

*A **single API** (OpenAI-compatible) in front of dozens of providers: route by price/perf, switch to a **fallback** if a provider goes down, pool keys, budgets and observability. A building block directly tied to **cost control** and to a product's multi-provider resilience. Background concept: [LLM observability — best practices](../concepts/llm-observability-best-practices.md).*
> **LLM-cost reading key**: two models stand opposed. **Self-host / pass-through** gateways ([LiteLLM](../tools/litellm.md), [Portkey](../tools/portkey.md)) do **not** bill tokens — you keep your direct relationship with the provider (🔑 BYOK). **Hosted** gateways ([OpenRouter](../tools/openrouter.md), [Requesty](../tools/requesty.md)) may **resell inference** with fees/margin (💸), while often offering a BYOK mode (🔑).
